Iterations:

1. Create a material readiness dashboard for team leaders, so they can check material shortages for upcoming work packages and record an escalation for the relevant stakeholders.

2. Notify relevant stakeholders by email or IM when a shortage is escalated. Provide a dashboard where they can see packages that need materials and track replenishment.

3. Add stock allocation or pre-allocation across packages, to help prevent conflicts when several packages need the same limited stock.

4. Let team leaders or relevant stakeholders submit candidate alternative materials for review. Track the review and approval status before an alternative is used. A higher specification or catalogue match alone does not establish suitability or compliance. The approval process needs careful design because requirements may depend on the project and consent.
