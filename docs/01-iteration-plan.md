Iterations:

1. As a Team Leader, 
I want to check material shortages for the selected planned work packages before going on site in a planned work packages dashboard, see the material shortage details and export the result as a CSV snapshot to keep as a record or to share with stakeholders outside the system, 
So that I can identify the material shortages, notify stakeholders, and reschedule crews.

2. As a Team Leader or relevant stakeholder,
I want to escalate and notify relevant stakeholders by email or IM when there is a shortage for the targeted planned work packages, and provide a dashboard where they can see packages that need materials and track replenishment,
So that stakeholders are immediately aware of escalated shortages and can centrally monitor the status of material replenishment.

3. As a Team Leader or inventory manager,
I want to add stock allocation or pre-allocation across packages,
So that we can help prevent conflicts when several packages need the same limited stock.

4. As a Team Leader or relevant stakeholder,
I want to submit candidate alternative materials for review, and track the review and approval status before an alternative is used—knowing that a higher specification or catalogue match alone does not establish suitability or compliance, and that the approval process needs careful design because requirements may depend on the project and consent,
So that we can ensure all alternative materials undergo a rigorous, compliant, and project-specific review process before they are deployed on site.
